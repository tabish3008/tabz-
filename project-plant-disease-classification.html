Plant Disease Classification

Personal Project • 2025
Scroll

Overview

This is an end-to-end computer vision model that looks at a photo of a leaf and tells you which disease, if any, the plant has. It covers a range of crops and plant diseases.

How it works

  • OpenCV cleans up each image first: it reduces noise, converts the colors and uses thresholding to isolate the infected area of the leaf.
  • A convolutional neural network (CNN), built and tuned in TensorFlow, learns to classify the disease from the prepared images.

Result

The model reached 85% classification accuracy under varying lighting conditions.

Built with

Python, TensorFlow and OpenCV.

Topics: computer vision, deep learning
