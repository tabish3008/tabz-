Sea Route AI

Personal Project • 2026
Scroll

Overview

Sea Route AI is a simulation of how an autonomous ship can find a safe and efficient route at sea. It looks at how the ship moves, the weather around it, and the rules ships must follow to avoid collisions.

What it does

  • Simulates the ship’s motion and guidance, predicting its telemetry as it moves.
  • Builds in the International Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea (COLREGs).
  • Plans paths in real time and computes the shortest route using live weather conditions.
  • Checks safe maneuvering envelopes in busy shipping corridors.
  • Draws the ship’s trajectories with Matplotlib so the results are easy to analyse.

Research paper

I wrote this work up as a paper, “Sea Route AI: Intelligent Maritime Route Optimization Framework”, and submitted it to the International Journal of Engineering Research & Technology (IJERT). Paper ID: IJERTV15IS042311.

Built with

Python and Matplotlib.

Topics: machine learning, simulation, research
